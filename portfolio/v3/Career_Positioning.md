# Career positioning | NYU M.S. in Management & Analytics

A commercially minded analyst who combines financial modeling, business intelligence, and data analytics to support profitable business decisions. This independent portfolio uses a fictional beauty catalog and simulated finance. The project does not imply employer affiliation, access to proprietary data, or realized impact.

## Commercial and merchandising analytics

- Built cohort-scoped product and SKU coverage, brand/category rankings, and native-currency price ladders for a synthetic beauty catalog.
- Used eligible sale observations and a contribution model to compare 15% and 10% offer alternatives, quantify break-even, and recommend a test gate.
- Presented assortment, pricing, and campaign decisions in a ten-page Power BI report and a commercial executive memo.

**LinkedIn project copy:** An independent commercial analytics case explores what assortment and pricing signals can support, then uses simulated campaign contribution to set an approval threshold. Includes native-currency reporting and a Streamlit promotion walkthrough.

## Finance and FP&A

- Built a driver-linked 12-month P&L and scenario workbook with Base, Bull, and Bear, weighted margins, and five reconciliation checks.
- Reconciled 36 scenario-month forecasts, 60 budget variance rows, and 12 price–volume–mix bridges using Python and the Excel model.
- Evaluated SKU and channel contribution and 35 campaign combinations to explain the difference between revenue uplift and modeled economics.

**LinkedIn project copy:** A driver-based planning case connects modeled revenue, gross profit, operating expenses, and operating profit. It includes budget variance, scenario analysis, promotion ROI, and a reconciliation trail from Python to the workbook.

## Business analytics and BI

- Developed portfolio evidence for a ten-page Power BI project with 76 visuals, 28 tables, 32 DAX measures, and auditable cohort and scenario context.
- Used Python, SQL, and SQLite to validate source lineage, metrics, coverage, and conservative item matching before report publication.
- Documented validated report bindings and workbook controls and captured reproducible scenario and promotion interactions in Chromium.

**LinkedIn project copy:** A governed retail analytics workflow with a Python/SQLite pipeline, Power BI semantic model, and interactive Streamlit public demo. The case makes scope, denominators, and unavailable comparisons visible to stakeholders.
