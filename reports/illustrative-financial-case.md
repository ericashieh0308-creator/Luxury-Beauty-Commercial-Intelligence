# Illustrative decision brief | Premium beauty retailer

**Evidence classification: simulated.** Calculations come from `beauty.finance` defaults and the generated workbook. Prices, units, channel shares, and costs are assumptions.

| Twelve-month case | Net revenue USD | Operating profit USD |
|---|---:|---:|
| Base | 700,824 | 155,493 |
| Bull | 771,141 | 204,022 |
| Bear | 586,266 | 68,604 |

The downside combines an 8% volume contraction, 9% unit-cost inflation and a five-point increase in discount versus baseline assumptions. At the modeled cost structure, annual operating profit falls about $86,889 from Base. Review procurement and price guardrails before committing fixed costs.

At a 15% promotional discount, a 20% modeled unit uplift and a $1,500 campaign cost, net revenue rises $6,110 while contribution falls $455. The resulting ROI is −30.3%. Gross margin declines by about 3.17 percentage points. The breakeven threshold is about 1,218.05 orders, or 1,219 whole units, equal to roughly 21.8% modeled uplift before rounding.

**Management action.** Stage spend behind a contribution threshold. Use order and return data, paid media incrementality, actual landed costs and fulfillment costs to validate inputs before approving the offer.
